FAQ

Straight answers

How does payment work?
Fixed packages are typically 50% to start and 50% on delivery, unless we agree otherwise in writing. Care plans are monthly in advance. Banking details come on the invoice — never in a first cold email.
Who owns the work?
You own the deliverables we build for you once the final invoice is paid, except for open-source libraries and tools that keep their own licences. I may reuse general patterns, not your private data or brand assets.
How is personal data handled (POPIA)?
Builds that touch customer data follow South Africa’s POPIA: minimise what we store, consent where needed, and no silent marketing lists. Site forms never auto-email you. Read the privacy notice.
What support do I get after launch?
Each build includes a short handoff window for bugs in the agreed scope. Ongoing hosting, monitoring and small tweaks are the optional care plan — or a new fixed quote for larger changes.
Are the prices final?
The ranges are real starting points. Where you land depends on scope, and your written quote after a call is the binding number.
