## Timing (~90 seconds)

### 0:00–0:15 Hook
Hi, is that Thandi? Sorry to catch you mid-day — I’ll be quick. My name is Katlego from Mens Mercatus.
We build one concierge desk that holds an agency’s whole digital footprint — and sells your work before the call even ends.
Could I have 90 seconds to show you what that looks like for an agency like yours?

### 0:15–0:45 Demo walk — open Concierge
I’ll send you a link — open it on your phone. This is the Concierge canvas: mensmercatus.online/apps/makedas-canvas.
Top of the screen — “Talk with Assist”. Ask it anything about the product or policy; it answers live. That’s your front desk that never goes to lunch.
Scroll down. Ops Launch — your runbook for every new client, in one place. Role Kits — role-ready packs for each placement or brief, so your consultants aren’t rebuilding the same pack every week.

### 0:45–1:05 Desks that win the brief
Client Portal — your client sees status, assets and decisions without a WhatsApp chase. Launch Path and Deploy Path — how a demo becomes the live build, without the fog.
Meeting Desk — walkthroughs that stay on product. And Workspace — your day desk: files, Assists, next move.
That’s the whole idea: your digital footprint, one concierge.

### 1:05–1:30 Pilot close
Here’s what I suggest: a two-week pilot on one desk — Client Portal or Role Kits, whichever hurts most right now.
Who besides you signs off on something like this?
Can I book a Meeting Desk walkthrough with you and them — Tuesday or Thursday?
I’ll send the playbook PDF and the Concierge link right after this call.
