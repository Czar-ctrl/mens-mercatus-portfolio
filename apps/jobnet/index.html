JobNet Application · Career pipeline · Preview

A pipeline that thinks
before it drafts.

One app for the whole loop. JobNet scrapes public LinkedIn and Glassdoor listings, ranks them against real CV evidence, and AI Hawk (formerly Drop Net) drafts a tailored cover note and CV synopsis for the role you pick. You review it, then copy it or forward it by email yourself. In the real pipeline drafts land in Gmail as drafts. Nothing is ever sent or applied for automatically.

Freelance mode points the same models at small businesses: it scores which ones have a real AI-automation problem worth solving, suggests the offer, and drafts outreach that a person reviews before anything is sent.

02

AI Hawk draft

The draft step that used to be the separate Drop Net page. AI Hawk writes a cover note and CV synopsis for one role, built only from facts on the published CV and case studies, with sources. Anything it can't back up stays a [placeholder] or is listed as a gap. It runs from templates on this device: no LLM call, nothing stored or sent.

        Application-outcome model

        06

        Inbox reply classifier

        Multinomial Naive Bayes (words + bigrams), trained in your browser on a small labelled set, then blended with embedding similarity when the model is loaded. It labels each reply as interview, rejection or acknowledgement and shows the cues it used.
