03 · Research system

Forex Mercatus Codex

A hosted Python terminal for charts, multi-market views, and a session-scoped paper bot — built so it cannot be mistaken for a brokerage.

Paper research desk

Open the paper desk

Challenge

Show market structure, multi-panel analysis, and a bot loop without letting a visitor believe they are placing a real order. The hard part is the boundary, not the chart.

Role and scope

Built, hosted, and bounded the desk

  • Built a Python and Highcharts desk covering Chart, Multi, Whales, Flow, and Bot.
  • Hosted it on the VPS. The process listens on port 8790 and is published over HTTPS.
  • Gave each browser tab its own session-scoped paper bot instead of one shared tape.
  • Fused public feeds (Yahoo, Kraken, Binance) and fell back to synthetic tape when a feed is unavailable.
  • Cut inventory and load path so cold start and static serving are clearer on an ~87 MB app.

Stack

Architecture

  1. 01

    Feeds

    Public market data, with synthetic tape when a source is down. A delayed snapshot, not a broker feed.

  2. 02

    Desk

    Chart, multi-symbol compare, flow and whale views, and a paper bot. Highcharts for the portfolio demo.

  3. 03

    Session

    One paper bot per browser tab. Cap of 24 sessions. Idle sessions expire after 2 hours.

  4. 04

    Policy

    paper_only is true. Sign-in and journal edits stay off on the public desk. No order can be placed.

60–90 second demo

  1. Open the desk and confirm the paper label before you touch a symbol.
  2. Pick one symbol, then compare a second market in the multi view.
  3. Run one paper action. It should change simulated state only.
  4. Open the health JSON and check paper_only, the symbol list, and the session cap.

AI behavior

  • The bot is a constrained research assistant on simulated state.
  • It can summarize retrieved data and propose a paper-only action.
  • A policy gate blocks live orders. The simulated state is explicit.
  • Useful provenance on a response: timestamp, data source, and whether the line is observation, analysis, or simulation.

Security

  • No broker credentials and no order route.
  • Public desk disables sign-in and journal edits.
  • Each visitor session is isolated from other visitors’ paper state.

Monitoring

  • Published health JSON: paper_only, symbols, data sources, session cap, idle TTL.
  • Symbols on that health snapshot include SPX, NQ, BTC, XAU, and EURUSD.
  • Data sources noted there include synthetic tape, Yahoo, Binance, CFTC, and Forex Factory.

Limits

  • This is simulated research, not a brokerage, not an exchange, and not advice.
  • Quotes are delayed public snapshots. A missing feed becomes synthetic tape, which is labelled.
  • Highcharts is used as a non-commercial portfolio demo.
  • Test suites are in the private source. The public check is the health endpoint plus the paper-only UI.
